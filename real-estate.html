What it does for you

Four jobs, done whether you are free or not.

Most agents lose business in the gaps: the call they could not take at a showing, the seller who never heard back, the past client who used someone else. KeyLink closes those gaps.

Win the listing

Show up to the appointment with a polished listing presentation and a CMA built from the comps you pick, ready in minutes instead of a night.

Launch the listing

Edited, MLS-ready photos, AI room edits and virtual staging, and a cinematic listing video, all in the Premiere package.

Catch every lead

Ava texts back every call you miss within seconds, and on higher plans answers live, so a buyer never moves on to the next sign.

Keep every client

Weekly updates go to your sellers automatically, and past clients hear from you before they need an agent again.

Your assistants

Three assistants. Each with one job.

Ava · calls and texts

Ava (say “AY-vuh”) texts back every missed call in your name and keeps the conversation going until you can step in. From Momentum up, she answers calls live, too.

Shelby · showings

Call Shelby from the car and read her your addresses and any set showing times. She plans the fastest order using live traffic, reads it back, and sends you the route.

Kai · real estate answers

Tap Ask Kai in the KeyLink app and ask out loud. Kai answers in writing, and reads it to you if you are driving.
