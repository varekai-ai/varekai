About

Built by a real estate agent, for real estate agents.

Joey Hughes and his wife ran a real estate business for 17 years and were inducted into the RE/MAX Hall of Fame. KeyLink comes from those years.

Why Joey built KeyLink

Agents don’t lose business because they don’t care. They lose it in the gaps: the call during a showing, the seller who hasn’t had an update, the past client who hasn’t heard from them in a while.

There are plenty of tools for those problems. But a tool still has to be learned, set up and used, and a busy agent may not have the time or the know-how. That’s the difference Joey wanted. Most companies hand you the tools and say, “Here you go, go do it.” With KeyLink, we’re doing it.

KeyLink isn’t a template, either. Every agent’s business is different. You tell us how you work on a one-time onboarding call, and we set KeyLink up around you.

KeyLink is built in Weatherford, Texas, and works with agents remotely. Joey also does in-person walkthroughs in Weatherford, Hudson Oaks, Springtown and Willow Park.
