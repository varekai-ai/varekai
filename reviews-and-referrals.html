Reviews & referrals

Your rating is the first thing a stranger checks. The referral is what nobody asks for.

Reviews decide whether you show up in the map pack and whether the person looking at three plumbers picks you. Referrals bring bigger jobs and cost nothing. Both depend on asking at the right moment, which is exactly the thing a busy owner never gets around to.

The problem

You do good work and then never mention it again.

The customer was delighted on Tuesday. By Friday they have forgotten, and you never asked. Meanwhile the one unhappy customer you had all quarter is the only person motivated enough to write anything.

Reviews move the map pack

Rating and volume are two of the strongest local ranking factors there are. This is the part of SEO you can influence this week.

Timing is everything

An ask that lands an hour after the job is finished converts. The same ask three weeks later does not.

Unhappy ones reach you first

A private route for bad experiences means you fix it in a text thread instead of reading about it in public.

Referrals are the bigger prize

A referred customer costs nothing to acquire, trusts you before you arrive, and argues about price far less.

How it works

Ask every time, without ever remembering to.

1

The job closes

Triggered from your CRM, your invoice, or a tap on your phone. No list to work through later.

2

Timed to land while the work is fresh, in your voice, with a link that takes one tap.

3

Unhappy replies get routed

Anything short of happy comes to you privately before it has a chance to become a public review.

4

Five stars get a second ask

The customers who just told you they love the work are the ones asked to pass your name along.
