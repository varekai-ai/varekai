Follow-up is easy to skip when you’re busy.

A seller who doesn’t hear from you starts to wonder what you’re doing. A past client who doesn’t hear from you may use someone else next time. Neither one means you did a bad job. It usually means you got busy.

KeyLink sends the regular touches for you and imports up to 5,000 of your contacts at setup. Voice notes and a contract deadline tracker help you stay on top of what’s happening with each client.
