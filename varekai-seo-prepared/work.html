Website design

Sites built for how each
industry actually sells.

A roofing customer and a med spa customer are not making the same decision, so they should not get the same website. These concepts show how we approach that.

Tree Service

Canopy Tree Care

Safety as the selling point

Tree work is bought on trust — a stranger with a chainsaw in your yard. The hero leads with the crew visibly roped in and helmeted, and the trust strip answers the four things homeowners actually worry about before price ever comes up.

HVAC

Summit Heating & Air

Seasonal urgency, upfront pricing

HVAC is half emergency, half maintenance. Two CTAs split those audiences from the first screen, and “Upfront Pricing” sits in the trust strip because surprise invoices are the industry’s reputation problem.

Roofing

Ridgeline Roofing

Storm-driven, inspection-first

Roofing leads spike after weather. The layout pushes free inspection over sales, because a homeowner with a damaged roof wants a diagnosis before a quote — and an inspection is a far easier yes.

Junk Removal

Jessie’s Junk Removal

Phone number above everything

Same-day work bought on impulse. The number sits in the header, the hero and the footer band — for this business a phone call outperforms a form, so the design stops trying to be clever about it.

Auto Repair

Precision Auto Care

Countering distrust directly

Independent shops fight the perception of being upsold. “Honest repairs” is the headline and the entire trust strip is about communication and estimates — the objection is met before it is raised.

Landscaping

Grant & Sons Landscaping

Sell the finished picture, not the service list

Landscaping is bought with the eyes — nobody shops it on price first. The hero is a full-bleed evening shot of a completed yard, services shrink to a single icon strip, and the gallery is pushed high because the portfolio is the pitch.

Spa & Wellness

Stillwater Massage & Spa

The design has to feel like the service

Nobody books a massage from a page that shouts. Generous whitespace, a serif face and muted sage — the restraint is the argument. A trades layout here would actively repel the customer.

Real Estate

Alden Estates

Property search is the product

Luxury buyers browse before they enquire, so the search bar overlaps the hero and listings come before the agent. Editorial typography does the positioning work a price tag would cheapen.
