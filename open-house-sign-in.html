Open house sign-in for agents

Open house sign-in without a clipboard or an iPad.

Welcome Mat gives you a print-ready sign with a large QR code. Visitors scan it and sign in on their own phones, and Ava follows up with the home details.

Don’t let open house visitors leave without following up.

Paper sign-in sheets get messy and handwriting is hard to read. Handing visitors your iPad is awkward for both of you. With Welcome Mat, people sign in on their own phones, and you stay focused on talking with them.

After they sign in, Ava introduces herself as your AI assistant, thanks them for stopping by, sends the home details and asks if there’s anything she can help with. Holding the same house open again? Tap Run it again.
