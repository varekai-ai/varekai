Missed-call text-back for agents

Can’t answer during a showing? Ava texts them back in seconds.

A buyer calls about your sign while you’re with a client. Before they call the next agent, they get a text from Ava and a real conversation starts. It’s part of the KeyLink Foundation plan.

Book a free walkthrough Call Ava, (817) 686-3436

You can’t answer every call. Buyers don’t always wait.

You’re at a showing, on a listing appointment or at dinner with your family. A caller who gets voicemail may not leave a message, and may simply try another sign. Calling back later doesn’t help much if they’ve already reached someone else.

With KeyLink, the missed call starts a text conversation right away. Ava finds out what they need, answers what she can and alerts you. When you’re free, you pick it up from there.

Missed call · 2:14 PM
Hi Megan, this is Ava, Jordan’s AI assistant. Sorry we missed your call! How can I help?
I saw the sign at the house on Live Oak. Can I see it?
Happy to help with that! Would today or tomorrow work better?
