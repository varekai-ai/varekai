How it works

One call from you. We handle the rest.

Getting started with KeyLink doesn’t take a training course. Here’s what happens, from your walkthrough to your first week.

1

Book a walkthrough

Joey shows you KeyLink on one of your own listings, and you find the right plan together.

2

Have your onboarding call

This is your main job. You answer questions such as whether Ava should ask buyers if they have an agent, or schedule from your calendar.

3

We build your Ava

We set up your assistant, import up to 5,000 contacts and add your branding.

4

Scan the code

You get your personal KeyLink link by QR code, with nothing to type.

5

KeyLink goes to work

Ava covers your calls, and your tools and follow-up are ready. Order a Premiere whenever you have a listing.

What stays the same

Your phone number doesn’t change. Your plan is month to month with no contract. The one-time build fee covers setting up your own Ava.
