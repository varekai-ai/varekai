The problem

Most local ad spend fails for the same four reasons.

It is rarely the creative. It is what happens before and after the click.

Boosting instead of advertising

The boost button optimizes for engagement, not leads. You get likes from three states away and no phone calls. A real lead campaign is built in Ads Manager with a conversion objective.

Nobody calls the lead back

Facebook leads go cold faster than any other source. If the first contact is tomorrow morning, you paid for a name and nothing else.

No idea what a lead costs

Without conversion tracking and call attribution, you cannot tell a $14 lead from a $90 one, so you cannot tell which campaign to scale and which to kill.

The page has nothing on it

Someone sees the ad, checks your profile, and finds three posts from last spring. The ad worked; the credibility check did not.

How it works

How we run it.

1

Offer and audience first

We start with what you want more of — the job type with the best margin — then build audiences around your actual service radius, not a thirty-mile default.

2

Creative built for the feed

Photo and short-form video from your real jobs, written to stop the scroll and say what you do, where, and for whom. Multiple variations so we can see what works.

3

Wire the follow-up

Every lead form submission triggers an instant text and a call task within sixty seconds, routed into the same system your calls and web forms use.

4

Read the numbers weekly

Cost per lead, cost per booked job, which creative and which audience. We cut what loses and put budget behind what wins.
