Listing presentations and CMAs

A polished listing presentation and CMA, ready in minutes.

Upload the comps you picked. KeyLink reads them and builds a branded CMA and listing presentation, so you can walk in ready without giving up a whole night.

Book a free walkthrough Call Ava, (817) 686-3436

You pick the price. KeyLink helps you back it up.

Agents often do several CMAs before winning a listing, and each one eats up time. In KeyLink, you upload the MLS comp sheet as a PDF or photos, and AI reads the comps for you.

KeyLink doesn’t set your list price. You do. The CMA shows the seller why your price makes sense, with comp cards, a comp map, price per square foot and a side-by-side table. You can dictate your reasoning, and KeyLink tidies it up without changing what you meant.

Branded to you

Presentations carry your or your broker’s logo and templates, provided when you start.

Your marketing plan included

The deck covers how buyers shop, your marketing plan and launch week, not just the numbers.

Built at the home

Gather photos, price and details at the house. A voice walkthrough and MLS sheet upload fill in the facts and flag mismatches.
