The Approach

From first call to live automation in under a month.

Most automation projects stall in planning. Ours don't — here's the timeline we hold ourselves to on a typical engagement.

WEEK 1

Assess & scope

We map your workflows as they really run and scope the single automation with the fastest, clearest payoff — not the biggest, the fastest.

WEEKS 2–3

Build & integrate

We build directly into the tools your team already uses, so there's no separate system to adopt and no workflow to relearn.

WEEK 4

Launch & measure

Live in production, with the metric it was built to move tracked from day one — so you know it's working, not just running.

Services

Where we typically start

Most engagements begin in one of these four places, depending on how much groundwork is already done.

01

AI Readiness Assessment

A structured audit of your operations to identify the two or three places AI will pay for itself fastest — with a plan, not just a report.

02

Automation Implementation

Hands-on build of the agents, integrations, and workflows identified in assessment — deployed into your existing tools, not a parallel system.

03

Advisory & Change Management

Ongoing guidance for leadership and teams as AI changes how work gets done, so the system survives past the initial rollout.

04

Impact Measurement

Clear before/after metrics tied to the business outcome the project was meant to move — time saved, error rate, throughput, cost.
