Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Varekai collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers varekai.ai, keylink.varekai.ai, the KeyLink app, and the phone, text and voice assistants we run (including Ava, Shelby and Kai). “Varekai,” “we” and “us” mean Varekai, based in Weatherford, Texas.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, email, phone number and anything you write in a form, email, call or text.
  • Account information for KeyLink customers: the phone numbers you ask us to recognize, your home and office addresses for route planning, and your preferences.
  • Calls and messages: when you call or text one of our assistants, we keep the call details, recordings or transcripts, and the messages, so the assistant can do its job and we can fix problems.
  • Addresses you give Shelby: the showing addresses and times you read to her, used to plan your route.
  • Basic website data: standard server logs such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To answer your request and provide the services you signed up for.
  • To send the messages you asked for, such as showing routes, appointment confirmations and account notices.
  • To plan routes and drive times (addresses are sent to our mapping provider for that purpose only).
  • To keep our services secure and working, and to meet legal obligations.

Text messages and mobile numbers

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and the providers that deliver our messages, is permitted; all other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See the SMS terms for details.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our services, under agreements that limit their use of it to providing those services to us. These include our CRM and messaging platform, our voice assistant platform, our mapping provider and our website host. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the services, then delete or de-identify it unless we must keep it longer by law.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing automate@varekai.ai.
  • Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, and use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
