AI voice agents

An AI that answers the phone on the first ring, at 2am.

Not a menu tree. Not an answering service that takes a message. A voice agent that understands what the caller needs, answers their questions, and puts the job on your calendar while you sleep. Call it yourself before you decide anything.

The problem

Voicemail is where jobs go to die quietly.

An answering service takes a message you still have to act on. Voicemail is worse. Meanwhile the caller has moved on to the next name on the list, and you never find out they called at all.

It never sleeps

Storm damage at 11pm, a burst pipe on Sunday. The most urgent calls are the ones that arrive when nobody is in the office.

It never puts them on hold

No queue, no menu, no hold music. It picks up on the first ring every time, including when six people call at once.

It books, not just answers

A message in an inbox is still work for you. This one asks what the job is, checks your calendar and confirms the slot.

Your team stays on the tools

Nobody is climbing off a roof to answer a phone, and nobody is paying a receptionist to do it either.

How it works

From first ring to booked job.

1

The call comes in

To your existing number. Nothing about how customers reach you changes.

2

It answers and listens

In a voice you picked, trained on your services, your pricing and your rules.

3

It handles the job

Answers the usual questions, qualifies the work, and offers real times from your live calendar.

4

You get the booking

Plus a recording and a full transcript, so you can see exactly what was said.
