The problem

Ranking on page one is not the same as getting the call.

Here is what usually stands between a local business and the searches happening a few miles away.

Your Google profile is thin

Wrong categories, no service list, five photos from 2019, and a review count your competitor doubled last year. The map pack is decided mostly here, not on your website.

One page for twelve services

If you sell twelve things and have one page, Google has one thing to rank. Every service you sell needs its own page written around how people actually search for it.

No local signals anywhere

No city names in your headings, no service area on the page, no structured data telling search engines where you work. You look like a generic business to a very local search.

Invisible to AI answers

When someone asks ChatGPT for a plumber in Fort Worth, it pulls from directories, reviews and clean site structure. Most local sites give it nothing to quote.

What the first ninety days look like.

2

Structured data, FAQ markup, crawler access for GPTBot and the others, and citations in the directories that AI tools actually pull from.

Questions

The things owners actually ask.

Google Business Profile changes can move within a couple of weeks. Page rankings typically take 60 to 90 days to show real movement, longer in a competitive category like roofing or legal. Anyone promising page one in thirty days is selling you something.

No, and neither can anyone else — Google does not sell that and does not accept bids for it. What we can commit to is the work: the audit, the fixes, the pages, the profile, and honest monthly reporting on where you stand.

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews for a local business, the answer is assembled from sources it can read and trust: your site structure, your reviews, your directory listings. We make sure those exist, are consistent, and are readable by the crawlers those tools use.

Maybe not urgently. But word of mouth now runs through search too — someone gets your name from a neighbor and immediately looks you up. If what they find is thin, you lose a referral you already earned.
