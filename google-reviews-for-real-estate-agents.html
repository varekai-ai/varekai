Google reviews for real estate agents

More Google reviews without having to chase them.

KeyLink sends review requests and reminders, gives you a QR code and monitors new reviews. Ava drafts your replies, and on Autopilot they’re posted for you.

Happy clients mean to leave a review. Then life happens.

Closing day is busy, and asking for a review is easy to forget. Even a client who loved working with you can forget by the following week. A simple system asks at the right time and follows up.

It also helps people find you. KeyLink looks after your Google Business Profile at the level your plan includes, from a setup review on Foundation to monthly checks and posts on Autopilot.

Requests and reminders

Clients get asked and reminded, so you don’t have to keep track.

QR code

Replies drafted
