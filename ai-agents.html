The problem

You answer the same twelve questions forever.

Here is the pattern in most small businesses we look at.

The same questions, all day

Do you service my area. How much for a basic one. Are you open Saturday. Do you take cards. Each one is thirty seconds, and there are forty of them a day.

Questions arrive after hours

Well over half of inquiries come in outside business hours. A form that promises a reply tomorrow is competing against a competitor who answers tonight.

Real leads buried in noise

Tire-kickers, wrong-area requests and vendor pitches mixed in with the two people who are ready to buy. Sorting takes as long as selling.

Knowledge lives in one person's head

When the person who knows the pricing and the policies is out, everything slows down. Nothing is written anywhere a system can use.

How it works

How we build one.

1

We capture what you know

Pricing rules, service area, policies, common objections, what you will and will not take on. This gets written down properly — often the first time it ever has been.

2

Train, then test hard

The agent is trained on that material, then we run it against real past conversations and deliberately awkward questions to see where it gets things wrong.

3

Set the boundaries

We define exactly what it may answer, what it must never quote, and when it hands off to a person. Conservative by default — it says it will check rather than guessing.

4

Deploy and keep tuning

It goes live on your site, text line and messages. We review real transcripts weekly for the first month and tighten anything that reads wrong.
