The problem

Your site is probably losing people in the first five seconds.

Over half of local searches happen on a phone, usually with a customer who has a problem right now. Here is where those visits leak out.

It loads too slowly

Every extra second of load time costs conversions. Big hero images, bloated page builders and unoptimized photos are the usual culprits — and they are all fixable.

It breaks on a phone

Text too small to read, buttons too close to tap, a menu that covers the screen. If someone has to pinch and zoom to find your number, they go back to the search results.

There is no obvious next step

A homepage that explains who you are but never says what to do. No visible phone number, no booking button above the fold, no form that takes under thirty seconds.

Google cannot read it properly

Missing page titles, duplicate headings, no schema markup, no service pages. The site exists, but search engines have nothing specific to rank.
