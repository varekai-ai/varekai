Virtual staging for real estate agents

Show what a room could be, right from your phone.

Snap a room in the KeyLink app, choose your edits and generate them at once. It’s useful for listing photos, at the listing appointment, or when a buyer can’t see past the clutter.

Room edits are currently in pilot.

Help people see past what’s there now.

A seller doesn’t want to hear that the pink bedroom needs paint, but they may understand when they see it. A buyer may love a house once they can picture it without someone else’s furniture. Room edits let you show them on the spot.

You can pick several changes and generate them together, then keep editing the result. Staging comes in styles including Transitional, Contemporary, Modern Farmhouse, Scandinavian, Coastal, Mid-Century, Modern Luxe, Industrial and Rustic.
