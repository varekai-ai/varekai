AI assistant for real estate agents

One assistant for real estate agents. Her name is Ava.

Ava handles the calls you can’t take, plans your showing day and answers real estate questions when you need them. You deal with one assistant, not a stack of apps.

Book a free walkthrough Call Ava, (817) 686-3436

Agents don’t need another app to log in to.

Most tools hand you software and leave the work to you. Ava is set up for you, and you reach her the way you already work: by phone, by text, or by talking into the KeyLink app.

She always tells people she’s your AI assistant, so nobody is misled. Her first text says so, and you decide how she handles things during your one-time onboarding call. For example, should she ask a buyer whether they already have an agent? Should she schedule from your calendar? Every agent’s business is different, so Ava isn’t a template.

Calls and texts

Ava texts back every call you miss and keeps the conversation going until you can step in. From Momentum up, she answers calls live too.

Showing routes

Call Ava from the car and read her your addresses and any set showing times. She plans the order and sends you the route.

Real estate answers

Tap Ask Ava in the KeyLink app and ask out loud about contract terms, deadlines or quick math. She answers in writing and can read it to you while you drive.
